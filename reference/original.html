Before you go

Can I book just an Innova Crysta or private taxi for Spiti?

Yes. You can request private transport without a hotel package. Vehicle availability and suitability are confirmed for your dates and group.

Does this itinerary operate throughout the year?

No. Kunzum, Chandratal and the Manali side of the full circuit are seasonal. When access is closed or unsuitable, ask about a Kinnaur-side return from Kaza.

Can we change the sightseeing stops?

Yes. This is a sample itinerary. Stops such as Gue, Chitkul and Pin Valley depend on the available time, road access and your preferences.
