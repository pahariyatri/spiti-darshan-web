# Spiti Darshan: brand and enquiry guide

## What we sell

An Innova Crysta road trip with a driver for the traveller’s group. The route is planned around their dates; homestays are a separate option. The website introduces the experience and price. WhatsApp is where the full plan, inclusions and booking are agreed.

Positioning: **Your Spiti trip, planned with you.**

## Visitor questions

These are design hypotheses, not findings from customer interviews:

- Is this the trip I want? Show real destination photography and the experience.
- What am I paying for? State car price for the group; separate homestay pricing.
- Who am I dealing with? Use the Spiti Darshan name and real fleet photos. Keep vehicle and team details available on supporting pages.
- What should I do next? One clear invitation to get a trip plan on WhatsApp.
- Am I ready to book? Asking for a plan starts a conversation; confirm details before booking.

The original hero text is owner-approved and should remain unchanged unless explicitly requested.

## Landing-page order

Hero → winter offer (with the sample winter route) → the whole route → real vehicle → booking questions → WhatsApp invitation.

The page is a scroll story: the visitor sees the offer, drives the whole route on the map, meets the real car, then sees exactly what one WhatsApp message gets them. Stops are named plainly (no “optional” labels); timing and access are handled in the WhatsApp plan. Stop clicks stay on the route. Keep team biographies and extended destination guides off the main conversion path.

## Less-is-more copy rules

- Give each section one job. Use a headline, one short explanation and an action where needed.
- Say “car price for your group,” not an ambiguous “package from” price.
- Use “Get my trip plan on WhatsApp” for general enquiries; preserve route/day context for specific enquiries.
- Keep practical qualifications next to the offer they explain.
- Use breakfast and dinner rather than relying on “MAP” alone.
- Avoid invented ratings, guarantees, urgency, discounts or response times.
- Real fleet and destination photos build the story; avoid generic travel-agency claims.

## Pricing item to resolve

The owner supplied ₹1,500 per person for homestay with breakfast and dinner, but has not confirmed whether this is per night or for the trip. Current copy preserves “per person.” Confirm the charging period and then state it explicitly beside the price and in the WhatsApp package. Do not infer it.

## Long-term improvement

- Review actual enquiries for repeated questions and answer only the recurring ones on the page.
- Maintain one current package sheet for WhatsApp with dates, route, car, stays, inclusions, exclusions and booking terms.
- Ask permission to use real guest feedback and trip photos; add proof when available.
- Refresh seasonal offers and availability as operations change.
- Existing click tracking records CTA placement and campaign tags; GA4 requires configuration. A WhatsApp click is not a sent message or a booking.
- Track received enquiries, qualified enquiries, quotes and bookings separately in the business workflow. Compare these with site visits and WhatsApp clicks.
- Test one meaningful change at a time. Keep changes that improve qualified enquiries and bookings, rather than judging only by click volume.
